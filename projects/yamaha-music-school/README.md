## Project Overview

**Knowledge Leverage Strategy** — A proof-of-concept partnership between José Carlos (JotaPuntoCe) and Yamaha Music School to:

1. **Solve your tuition problem** — Get music classes for free by implementing a growth system
2. **Serve an untapped market** — 350,000+ people in SLP who want to learn music but can't pay
3. **Build a sellable method** — Document the process as JotaPuntoCe's flagship product
4. **Create a revenue stream** — Scale via licensing to other schools/businesses
